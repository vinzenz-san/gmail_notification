Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-31

Gmail Notification is a browser extension (Firefox & Chrome) that watches one or more Google accounts you're already logged into in your browser and notifies you when new mail arrives. This policy explains what the extension accesses and why.

Summary

  • The extension does not operate its own server, does not use analytics, and does not transmit any of your data anywhere.
  • There is no OAuth sign-in, no Google Cloud project, and your Gmail password or credentials are never entered into or seen by the extension.
  • It reads Gmail's own per-account, undocumented unread-mail Atom feed (mail.google.com/mail/u/<n>/feed/atom/) directly in your browser, authenticated with the session cookies you already have from being logged into Gmail — the same mechanism unread-count bookmarklets have used for years. This is a request from your device straight to Google, not through any server of ours.
  • The unread-mail data this reveals (sender, subject, a short snippet, and a link) is used only to show the popup preview, the toolbar badge count, and the notification/sound — and is cached in your browser's local extension storage. None of it leaves your device.
  • Your settings (which accounts to watch, their order, and the notification/sound/badge/theme toggles) are stored only in your browser's local extension storage (browser.storage.local), via the standard storage permission.
  • The extension only ever contacts mail.google.com. It does not run on, read, or access any other website.

Permissions

PermissionWhy it's needed
storage Saves which accounts to watch (and their order), the notification/sound/badge/theme settings, and a short-lived cache of each account's unread mail, so the popup can show it instantly.
alarms Schedules the recurring check for new mail (every 2 minutes) in the background, without needing a page or tab kept open.
notifications Shows a system notification when new mail arrives, if you've enabled that toggle. Clicking it opens the right account's inbox.
offscreen (Chrome only) Chrome's background service worker has no page/DOM to play a sound or parse feed XML with. This permission creates a hidden, invisible page in the background solely to do those two things. Firefox's background page can do both natively and never uses this.
Host access to mail.google.com Required to fetch each watched account's unread-mail feed using your existing browser session. The extension does not run on, read, or access any other site.

Third parties

Gmail Notification is an independent, unofficial project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. It does not share your data with Google, advertisers, or any other third party beyond the unread-mail feed request each account's fetch already makes directly to mail.google.com using your own browser session — the extension itself has no server and no analytics.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised and the change will be reflected in the extension's GitHub repository.
