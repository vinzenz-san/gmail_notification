Gmail Notification

A free browser extension for Firefox and Chrome that watches one or more logged-in Google accounts and notifies you when new mail arrives — without keeping a Gmail tab open.

Get the add-on forFirefox

What it does

Gmail Notification reads the unread-mail count and subject/sender for every Google account you're already logged into in your browser, checks every 2 minutes, and tells you when something new shows up — with a popup preview, a sound, and/or a toolbar badge.

Multiple Gmail accounts at once

Detects every currently logged-in Google account and lets you pick which ones to watch — reorder them anytime, the order persists.

Popup preview, no Gmail tab needed

Click the toolbar icon to see the latest unread mail per account — sender and subject — and open one directly. Click an account's name to open its inbox even with nothing unread.

Notification, sound, and badge — each optional

Toggle a popup notification, a short sound, and an unread-count badge on the toolbar icon independently. One background check for the whole browser, so multiple open windows never trigger duplicate sounds.

Private, no OAuth, open source

No sign-in, no Google Cloud project, no password ever entered or seen. It reads Gmail's own per-account unread-mail feed using your browser's existing session — nothing leaves your device. Free and open source under the MIT license.
